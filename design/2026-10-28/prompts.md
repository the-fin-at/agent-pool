# Agent Pool · Design-Prompts

Erstellt am 28. Oktober 2026 mit dem eingebauten Imagegen-Tool. Fünf unabhängige UI-Entwürfe mit jeweils eigenem Icon-Konzept. Reihenfolge entspricht den tatsächlich im Chat angezeigten Bildern. Keine UI-Implementierung vor der Auswahl.

## Entwurf 1

Datei: [01-porcelain.png](01-porcelain.png)

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a realistic, production-quality redesign concept named Porcelain for Agent Pool, a small desktop application on macOS and Windows that monitors connected AI CLI accounts and their usage limits. One independent design, a single focused primary screen, not multiple options or a collage.
Target Dimensions: 1440 x 1024 landscape desktop UI. Front-on crisp application content with a restrained desktop title bar, no browser chrome, no laptop or scenery, no perspective, no presentation background or device mockup.
Design direction: exceptionally clean light interface, warm-white base, dark charcoal text, restrained deep emerald accents, precise SF/Inter-like typography, confident whitespace. Quiet craft comparable to a polished professional native utility. Use alignment and subtle row separators instead of cards inside cards, very little border, almost no shadow.
Composition: a lean top app bar with a genuinely new custom Agent Pool app icon and wordmark at the left, small refresh control at right. Main heading "Accounts" with a short quiet status line "3 verbunden · Aktualisierung alle 60 s". Below, one beautiful spacious unified list/table containing three account rows, NOT a grid of dashboard cards. Each row clearly aligns account identity, "5 Stunden frei" bar and remaining percentage, "Woche frei" bar and remaining percentage, and next reset. These are the hero elements. One primary action "Aktualisieren". One supporting account search at most. Do not add fake analytics, token totals, money, charts, navigation, or features.
New original icon concept: a memorable minimal interlocking loop forming an abstract P/pool junction, two broad geometric loops in forest green on a pale mint rounded square. Large enough to judge at about 60px in the app bar, consistent with an app icon that remains distinctive at 16px; no generic AI sparkle, robot, chat bubble, or hamburger bars. Integrate this icon within this single product design; do not create a separate icon board.
UI text language: German. Visible texts should include "Agent Pool", "Accounts", "Aktualisieren", "5 Stunden frei", "Woche frei", "Nächster Reset". Readable 14-16px baseline, strong type hierarchy, no tiny decorative text.
Use fictional demonstration data, not real telemetry: Privat, privat@example.com, Pro, 72% free for five-hour window and 41% free weekly, reset in 2 Std. 20 Min.; Arbeit, arbeit@example.com, Business, 92% and 86% free, reset in 4 Std.; Team, team@example.com, Business, 18% and 12% free, reset in 45 Min. Make bars visually match remaining percentages, use amber only for the low account. Small footer "Beispieldaten". Today is 28 October 2026, Europe/Vienna; avoid other calendar dates. Distinguish free allowance from consumption clearly. Show a tasteful small connected indicator per row. Keep the entire screen focused, readable, uncrowded and implementable as real desktop UI.
